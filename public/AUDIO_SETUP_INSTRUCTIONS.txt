==================================================

The website is configured to play an ambient ocean soundscape file from:

AUDIO SPECIFICATIONS REQUIRED:
-------------------------------
- Duration: 10 minutes minimum
- Format: MP3, OGG, or WAV (MP3 recommended for web)
- Quality: 48kHz, 24-bit or 320kbps MP3
- Loop: Perfectly seamless (no clicks or pops at loop point)
- Content: Calm ocean waves, gentle pebble beach sounds
- Wave timing: Every 8-10 seconds (synced to 4-7-8 breathing)
- NO: Seagulls, wind, voices, boats, sudden sounds
- Features: Binaural recording feel, pink/brown noise undertones
